# Self-Check

Use when auditing whether `tutor` is working, for one course or across all of them. Not for loading during a session.

Each check is observable in the course files. A failing check points at the skill or the course, not at the learner.

- A session ends with something the learner produced; `sessions/` logs name that output.
- `ledger.md` grows faster than `maintenance/`. A course whose maintenance folder outgrows its ledger is being planned, not practiced.
- Unaided cold reps (delay 1+) appear, not only same-session rows.
- `Starting model` in `course.md` cites probe evidence, not only the learner's self-report.
- Claims in exact-procedure domains carry a source or were executed; teaching sessions name one primary source.
- Option-based questions spread the correct answer across positions rather than favoring one slot.
- `artifacts/glossary.md` exists once the course has terms the learner uses unaided, and later sessions use its terms.
- Concepts reach `reliable` only through unaided ledger rows, and later sessions stop re-teaching them.
- Each milestone's external check has happened or is scheduled, rather than quietly dropped.
- Courses with empty ledgers are `paused`, not left `active`.
